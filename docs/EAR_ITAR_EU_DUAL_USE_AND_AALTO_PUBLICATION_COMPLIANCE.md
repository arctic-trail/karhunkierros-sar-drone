## 1. Executive Verdict: Is Anything ITAR-Controlled?

- **ITAR (`22 CFR Parts 120–130`, USML): `ZERO RISK (0% ITAR Content)`.**
  - Nothing in `Opaslyhty` or `PELASTUS` falls on the U.S. Munitions List (`USML Cat VIII` or `Cat XII`). Commercial uncooled microbolometers (`Teledyne FLIR Boson` / `Lynred Atto`), standard civilian `u-blox M10` GNSS, and unarmed civil SAR airframes are governed by civil/dual-use rules (**U.S. EAR `15 CFR Parts 730–774`** and **EU Dual-Use Regulation `(EU) 2021/821`**), not ITAR.
