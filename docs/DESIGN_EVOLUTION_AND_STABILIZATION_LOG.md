## 4. "Uber-for-SAR" Open-Alert Marketplace, Roofed Auto-Stations & 28 m Elastic Pull-Guidance (`F23`–`F38`)

1. **Open-Alert Broadcast to Mobile Units & Roofed Auto-Stations (`broadcast_open_alert()`, `F29`):**
   - Enforces `verify_humanitarian_sar_trigger()` ("Pull, Don't Push" privacy gate, `F29`): fails closed (`REJECTED_NO_HUMANITARIAN_SAR_TRIGGER`) unless triggered by an authorized `112 Suomi`, `Garmin inReach`, Trail-Post SOS button, overdue *autiotupa* check-in, or official `Poliisi` / `Rajavartiolaitos` / `Pelastuslaitos` SAR order.
   - When authorized, broadcasts an open task (`OPEN_FOR_UNIT_ACCEPTANCE`) to all registered **Mobile Patrol Units** (`POLIISI-VAN-OULU-21`, `RAJA-PARTIO-KUUSAMO-04`, `VAPEPA-AHKIO-RUKA-07`) and **Automated Roofed Wilderness Stations** (`NEST-OULANKA-ROOF-01`, `NEST-VALTAVAARA-ROOF-02`).
2. **Weatherproof Roofed Stations (`+10 °C`) with Auto-Accept & Live Hardware Safety Interlocks (`F23`, `F30`, `F33`):**
   - Automated stations sit under a motorized weatherproof roof (`+10 °C` heated enclosure keeping snow, freezing rain, and rime ice off the airframe and keeping the `8,000 mAh` Li-Ion pack at `100%` warm capacity, while `evaluate_finnish_winter_flight_endurance()` decouples `cell_temp_c` from `ambient_temp_c` so the `+42 °C` cask heater and `40–45%` winter return reserve remain active in `-22 °C` air, `F32`).
   - When set to `AUTO_ACCEPT_GEOFENCE`, the station automatically accepts tasks within its `14 km` geofence **only if** its `F23` safety interlock passes (`roof_hatch_operational == True`, `roof_snow_load_kg <= 25.0 kg`, `internal_temp_c >= -15.0 °C`, and `auto_accept_mode != "MAINTENANCE_OFFLINE"`).
   - Live bid refresh (`_refresh_active_task_bids()`, `F33`) re-evaluates `self.fleet_units` before every prioritization, manual assignment, or backup promotion. If all accepting units are unfeasible or have jammed hatches, `auto_prioritize_and_award_task()` fails closed (`BLOCKED_NO_FEASIBLE_OR_SAFE_UNITS`) and never launches an unsafe unit.
3. **Multi-Unit Auto-Prioritization & Automatic `#2 Backup` Failover (`F24`, `F33`):**
   - When multiple units accept the same alert, `auto_prioritize_and_award_task()` scores every feasible bid (`0–100 pts`): **Flight ETA (`45%`)**, **Winter Battery Endurance Margin (`25%`)**, **Thermal/Cask/GNSS-Denied Capability (`15%`)**, and **Shelter/Optical-Diode Readiness (`15%`)**.
   - Ranks `#1` as `PRIMARY_SAR_RESCUER` and `#2` as `SECONDARY_BACKUP_INTERCEPT`. If `#1` aborts (`ABORT_PRIMARY_PROMOTE_BACKUP`), `#2` is verified against live `shelter_safety_interlock_ok and mission_feasible` before promotion (`F33`).
4. **Human Coordinator Control Panel & Governance Modes (`F31`, `F37`):**
   - Supports three governance modes: `AUTO_DISPATCH_WITH_HUMAN_VETO` (default), `MANUAL_COORDINATOR_APPROVAL_ONLY`, and `FULL_AUTONOMOUS_ZERO_TOUCH`.
   - At any moment in the 3D UI (`collector_hub/ui/index.html`) or API, the human coordinator can click **Force Assign Unit**, **Approve Manual Launch**, **Abort Primary & Promote #2 Backup**, **Nest #1 Auto: ON/OFF**, **Hold / Pause Launch**, **Force Immediate RTB**, or **Standoff Orbit**.
5. **Closed-Loop `28 m AGL` "Follow & Elastic Pull" Guidance with Hard-Coded `15 m` Standoff, Aft Gimbal Yaw & Lateral *Autiotupa* Clearing Arrival (`F25`, `F27`, `F28`, `F35`):**
   - After dropping the `+42 °C` berry-electrolyte cask and thermal blanket at a `15 m` horizontal offset, the drone climbs to **`28.0 m AGL`** (safely above `15–22 m` spruce crowns) and engages `compute_elastic_pull_guidance_vector()`:
     - **Hard-Coded `>= 15.0 m` Horizontal Airframe Standoff & Aft Gimbal Orientation (`F27`, `F35`):** The `2.1 kg` airframe never moves closer than `15.0 m` horizontally to the hiker (`enforce_hardcoded_human_safety_geofence()`). When closer spotlight guidance (`6.0 m` or `10.0 m` ahead of the hiker) is needed while the airframe is `15.0 m` ahead, the gimbal rotates aft (`spotlight_gimbal_yaw_rel_deg = 180.0`) and pitches (`spotlight_gimbal_pitch_deg`) toward the hiker so the illuminated snow pool lands `6.0 m` or `10.0 m` in front of their boots.
     - **`ARRIVED_AT_AUTIOTUPA_SAFE_HAVEN` Lateral Clearing Offset (`F35`):** Once within `20.0 m` of the *autiotupa*, the drone offsets `15.0 m` laterally perpendicular to the trail (`-uy_goal, ux_goal`) in the cabin's front clearing (`drone_distance_to_autiotupa_m >= 15.0 m`), illuminating the cabin door from the side without ever overflying the cabin roof or hot chimney before executing autonomous Return-to-Base (`RTB`).
